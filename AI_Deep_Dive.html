How answers are checked

Exact listed answers and aliases score instantly. Typos and unlisted answers are checked by the AI for category validity and then rarity. Valid answers score automatically; uncertain cases can be reviewed by the host. Wrong answers earn zero; rarity points apply only after an answer passes category validation. Other answers go to the local AI judge when you launch with START_GAME.bat or start_ai_game.py with Ollama running. The timer pauses while the local judge checks. English and Arabic answers are welcome. After the one-time model download, judging works offline. Leaderboard submission needs internet. If the judge is uncertain or unavailable, the host can award points. Review controls require the host PIN printed in the terminal each time the server starts. Players cannot award themselves points by clicking them.

Five everyday questions and two technical questions are selected each round. Scoring rewards estimated answer rarity among university students. A technical answer is not automatically rare. Scores are fixed during the event, so playing later gives no advantage. An answer does not have to appear in our examples to be valid.
